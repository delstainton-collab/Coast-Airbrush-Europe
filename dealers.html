Frequently Asked Commercial Questions

How is hazardous dangerous goods (ADR) transport handled?

All solvent products ship under UN1263 Class 3 Flammable Liquid classification via licensed hazardous freight partners (APC Overnight in the UK, Mainfreight across Europe) with fully compliant transport manifests and multi-lingual SDS.

How does zero-rated cross-border EU VAT work?

Approved EU commercial accounts with a verified cross-border VIES VAT number are automatically billed at 0% Intra-Community VAT from our Dutch bonded hub under EU reverse-charge mechanisms.

What are the Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)?

Authorized Tier 2 Dealers can order in single case packs (e.g. 6 bottles or 1 starter kit). Tier 1 Regional Distributors order in master pallet quantities with structured Net 30/60 day terms.

Do you provide counter staff training?

Yes. Every authorized stocking dealer receives complete digital application guides, staff cheat-sheets, and priority access to our master technical spray desk.
