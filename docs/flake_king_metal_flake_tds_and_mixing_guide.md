## 1. Product Description & Formulation
Flake King™ Polyester Flake is a high-precision, vacuum-metallized Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET) film engineered with proprietary thermoset protective coatings. Designed specifically for high-end automotive refinishing, marine, theme park, leisure, and industrial custom coating applications.

* **Material:** Coated Aluminum Metallized Polyester (PET) Film
* **Toxicity:** Formulated to be Non-Toxic (Heavy metal free)
* **Specific Gravity / Suspension:** Lower specific gravity than liquid/gel binders, ensuring uniform suspension during wet application and preventing sudden drop-out.

## 4. Advantages: Dry Application vs. Wet Application

While Flake King flakes can be wet-sprayed according to the table above, **dry application** using the Flake King 500, 550, or 1000 series dry guns is the factory-recommended technique for the following advantages:

1. **Substantial Material Savings:** Drastically cuts the volume of metal flake consumed per job.
2. **Clear Coat Reduction:** Eliminates gallons of unnecessary carrier clear coat.
3. **Zero Sagging / Clear Failure:** Eliminates heavy, sagging clear-and-flake build-ups.
4. **Massive Labor Time Savings:** Cuts **2 to 3 days** on a standard motorcycle/Harley-Davidson paint job and up to **a full week** on complete vehicle builds.
5. **Fast & Clean Dust-Off:** Excess dry flake simply blows or brushes off without sticky residue.
6. **No Equipment Cross-Contamination:** Clear coat guns stay completely clear-only, eliminating stray flake contamination on future paint jobs.

## 5. Technical Limitations & Precautions
1. **Exposed Aluminum Edge:** Cutting creates a micro-edge of exposed aluminum. Extreme prolonged contact with caustic or high-alkaline strippers should be monitored.
2. **Concentrated Sulfuric Acid:** Causes delamination of protective thermoset coating from the foil substrate.
3. **Experience Testing:** Always conduct test panel spray-outs with your selected clear coat, hardener, and reducer before commercial production.
