PRIVACY POLICY &
DATA PROTECTION NOTICE

Coast Airbrush Europe ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy, confidential business data, and personal information of our retail painters, custom automotive bodyshops, authorized stocking dealers, and website visitors. This notice details how we collect, process, store, and protect your information under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018), and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (EU GDPR).

folder_shared 1. Information We Collect

A. Customer Order & Logistics Data

When you purchase products (e.g., Kroma Edge chrome systems, Flake King guns, FK thinners, or consumable masking tapes), we collect your name, commercial bodyshop name, billing address, dispatch address, contact telephone number (mandatory for ADR hazardous courier delivery notices), and email address for shipment tracking.

B. Payment Information Security (Zero Local Card Storage)

All online payments are processed through tokenized, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways (such as Stripe and PayPal). Coast Airbrush Europe never receives, accesses, or stores your raw credit card numbers or security CVV codes on our servers.

C. B2B Wholesale & Authorized Dealer Credentials

For commercial stocking accounts and regional distribution applicants, we collect company registration documents, VAT/EORI identification numbers, trade bank references, and authorized purchaser email addresses to assess credit terms and configure wholesale tier pricing.

D. Technical Spray Desk Inquiries

When you contact our master spray technicians via our Support Desk, we retain nozzle calibrations, fluid pressure parameters, substrate notes, and ticket history to assist you across future batch applications.

gavel 2. Lawful Grounds for Processing

Under Article 6 of the GDPR, we only process personal information where a recognized lawful basis exists:

  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)): To execute, pack, clear customs, and deliver your orders, and manage wholesale supply contracts.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance (Art. 6(1)(c)): To maintain hazardous goods ADR transport manifests (UN1263 Class 3), comply with REACH and CLP chemical regulations, and maintain statutory HMRC / EU tax accounting records for 7 years.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To safeguard against credit card fraud, secure our master catalog database, hedge against cross-currency volatility spikes, and ensure supply chain stability.
  • Explicit Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For opt-in launch announcements, spray webinars, and special promotional announcements. Consent can be withdrawn at any time with a single click.

handshake 3. Third-Party Service Providers

cookie 4. Cookies & Local Storage

Coast Airbrush Europe employs cookies and browser local storage strictly for functional application integrity:

  • Active Cart Session: Retaining your selected paints, quantities, and pre-order tiers across navigation.
  • Currency & VAT Preferences: Preserving whether you view prices in GBP (£) or EUR (€) with applicable regional VAT breakdowns.
  • Authenticated Staff Sessions: Managing secure operator sessions inside the password-protected Admin Console.

We do NOT utilize invasive third-party ad-tracking pixels, social tracker canvases, or sell browsing habits to marketing broker networks.

shield 5. Your Legal Rights Under GDPR

You possess enforceable statutory rights concerning the personal data we hold about you:

Right of Access (DSAR) Request a free electronic copy of all personal records we store associated with your profile.
Right to Rectification Instruct us to correct outdated delivery addresses, contact numbers, or trade tax registrations.
Right to Erasure ("Forgotten") Request the deletion of your account and customer record, subject to 7-year statutory tax retention requirements.
Right to Object & Restrict Opt out of marketing communications or restrict processing during disputed data inquiries.

To exercise any of these rights, email our compliance officer at privacy@coastairbrush.eu. We will process your request within 30 calendar days without administrative fee.
