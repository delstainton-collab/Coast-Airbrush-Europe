ADR Dangerous Goods Logistics Standards

local_fire_department UN1263 Class 3 Classification

Products including FK100 Jet Black Basecoat, FK55 Fast Reactive Thinners, and Kroma Edge Chrome System contain volatile organic solvents classified as flammable liquids. Under European ADR treaty regulations, paints must travel via authorized dangerous goods vehicles with certified spill kits.

box Triple-Sealed UN Packaging

We package all liquid paints in leak-tested aluminium cans sealed with tamper-evident locking rings, wrapped in heavy-gauge polythene barrier sleeves, and packed with high-absorption vermiculite pillows inside heavy corrugated outer cartons.

draw Mandatory Signature on Delivery

Because our shipments contain hazardous chemicals, couriers are legally prohibited from leaving parcels unattended on doorsteps, in recycle bins, or in open carports. An adult signature (18+) is legally mandatory upon physical handover.

alarm Same-Day Dispatch Cutoff: 14:00 GMT

To allow our hazmat team sufficient time for UN manifest logging, ADR pallet consolidation, and courier verification, all orders placed before 14:00 GMT Monday through Friday are dispatched the same business day.
