Technical Operating Guides

Air Pressure & Viscosity Curve Matrix

The precision matched fluid nozzle utilizes Iwata micro-venturi channel geometry. Running excessive air pressure causes premature tip dry. Refer to our dynamometer calibrated matrix below:

Hairline Micro-Detail (0.5mm – 2mm width) 12 – 14 PSI | 60% Reduction
Soft Fog Gradients & Blends 16 – 18 PSI | 50% Reduction
Catalyzed Urethane Clearcoat Shading 20 – 24 PSI | 40% Reduction
info

End-of-Session Flush & Tear-Down

To preserve the hand-lapped mating surface between nozzle and needle shaft, follow the Coast Airbrush zero-torque disassembly ritual.

  1. Immediate Evacuation: Dump residual pigment and fill fluid cup halfway with compatible solvent or distilled cleaner.
  2. Crown Cap Backflush: Loosen crown cap by 1/2 turn, hold a lint-free shop towel firmly over tip, and depress trigger gently to route air bubbles back through the cup.
  3. Needle Extraction: Always withdraw needle toward the rear after unscrewing the chucking nut. Never pull forward through the body to avoid drawing pigment into the spring cage.
  4. PTFE Conditioning: Apply 1 micro-drop of synthetic PTFE lubricant to the needle shank before re-insertion.
warning

Critical Maintenance Warning

Never use pliers or metal forceps on the fluid nozzle. Hand-tighten using the provided precision Iwata wrench only until flush. Over-tightening will fracture the brass threads and void factory warranty.
