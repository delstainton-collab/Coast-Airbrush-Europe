tune Flake King: The 3 Golden Rules

DRY APPLICATION TDS
1. NEVER Spray at Standard Paint Pressure (5–10 PSI Only)

The Flake King is an airflow venturi gun, not an atomizer. High pressure (30–40 PSI) will blow flake everywhere and cause bounce-back. Dial your regulator to 5–10 PSI so flake drifts gently onto the wet clear.

2. The "Grip Coat" Method

Spray a light grip coat of 2K clear and let it flash 5 minutes. Apply a medium wet coat, then immediately spray your dry flake 12–15 inches away into the wet film.

3. Clean With Air (Zero Teardown)

Because no clear coat or liquid paint enters the gun barrel, there are zero fluid passages to clog. When finished, simply blow out the tube with dry shop air.

auto_fix_high Kroma Edge: Self-Organization Protocol

2K MIRROR TDS
Exact 4-Part Mixing Ratio: 5 : 5 : 2 : 2

Binder (5) : Reducer (5) : Hardener (2) : Mirror Seeds Formula (2). Always mix Binder and Reducer 1:1 first, then stir in Hardener and Mirror Seeds thoroughly.

Apply ONE Continuous Wet Coat

Do NOT apply mist or tack coats. Apply one full, continuous wet coat. The film will appear cloudy initially, then the metallic particles self-align to form a mirror within 60 seconds.

Clear Coating Rule

Allow to air cure for 36–48 hours (or force dry at 60°C for 1–2 hours). Apply Kroma Edge Dedicated Topcoat Clear. The finish remains 100% reflective mirror chrome.

info Why Flake King™ Strongly Recommends Dry Application:

  • Minimises flake usage: High transfer efficiency direct to wet binder.
  • Reduces clear coat volume: Avoids thick resin carrier overloading.
  • Eliminates sagging & failure: Prevents heavy flake-laden clear sagging.
  • Huge time savings: Saves 2-3 days on a motorcycle and up to 1 week on a car.
  • Effortless clean-up: Dry excess flake simply brushes or blows off.
  • Zero cross-contamination: Clear guns stay 100% flake-free for future jobs.
Physical & Quality Specifications

Composition: Precision vacuum-metallized Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET) film with thermoset protective coating.

Suspension: Lower specific gravity than liquid mediums ensures uniform suspension without rapid settling.

Adhesion Testing: Passed Cellophane tape strip test, 180° fold/crease test, and wrinkle test without delamination.

Quality Control: Multi-stage sieved screens to guarantee uniform particle geometry and eliminate oversized flakes.

Limitations & Handling Precautions

Exposed Aluminum Edge: Minute cut edges have exposed aluminum. Contact with aggressive caustics or strong alkaline strippers should be monitored.

Sulfuric Acid: Concentrated sulfuric acid will cause separation of the coating from the foil.

Experience Testing: Flake King™ flakes should be experience-tested in your target clear/resin system prior to production.
